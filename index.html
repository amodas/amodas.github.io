AI ENGINEER · APPLIED SCIENTIST

Hi, I'm Apostolos.

I work on machine learning research and engineering, with a focus on computer vision, multimodal AI, and model evaluation.

I'm currently a Senior AI Engineer at Sony AI, where I build evaluation methods, datasets, and tools for generative AI and responsible machine learning.

I've been at Sony AI since 2022, first as a Senior Research Scientist and now as a Senior AI Engineer. My work includes research that contributed to a Nature cover paper, open evaluation tools, and large-scale generative AI benchmarks.

I completed my PhD at EPFL, focusing on robustness and generalization in deep neural networks, and also worked on adversarial robustness at IBM Research. Before moving to Switzerland, I earned my MSc and BSc at the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens. I enjoy working across research and practical systems, especially where careful experiments can turn into useful tools.
